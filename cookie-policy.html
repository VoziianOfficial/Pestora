1. Overview

This Cookie Policy explains how Pestora may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies on this website. Pestora is an independent pest control provider matching platform and does not perform pest control services directly.

By using this website, you may be presented with a policy banner that allows you to accept or decline non-essential preference storage where applicable.

2. What Cookies and Local Storage Are

Cookies are small files placed on a browser or device by a website. Local storage is a browser-based storage method that can remember information such as a policy banner choice. Similar technologies may be used to support website functionality and user experience.

3. How We May Use These Technologies

Pestora may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies for limited website operations and experience improvements.

  • To remember whether you accepted or declined the policy banner.
  • To support basic website functionality and navigation.
  • To improve page performance and user experience.
  • To understand general site usage patterns where analytics tools are used.
  • To help maintain website security and reliability.

4. Policy Banner Storage

The website policy banner may store your choice in local storage so the banner does not appear repeatedly after you make a selection. This choice is stored in your browser and can be cleared by deleting browser site data.

5. Essential and Non-Essential Technologies

Essential technologies

Some technologies may be necessary for the website to work properly, including basic security, form usability, and preference storage.

Non-essential technologies

Non-essential technologies may include analytics or experience improvement tools. These may help us understand general website usage, but they are not required for the core provider matching request experience.

6. Managing Cookies

You can manage cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies, delete cookies, clear local storage, or receive alerts when cookies are being used.

Blocking certain technologies may affect website functionality, saved preferences, or form behavior.

7. Third-Party Technologies

Some website features, analytics tools, hosting providers, or service partners may use their own technologies. These third parties may process information according to their own privacy and cookie practices.

Pestora does not control the cookie practices of independent pest control providers or third-party websites that may be linked from this platform.
