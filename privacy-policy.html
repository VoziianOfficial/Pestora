1. Overview

Pestora is an independent pest control provider matching platform. Pestora helps homeowners submit request details and compare independent local provider options. Pestora is not a pest control company, does not perform pest control services directly, and does not send its own technicians.

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected when you visit this website, submit a request form, contact us, or interact with platform features.

2. Information We May Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide through forms, phone calls, email, or other website interactions.

  • Name and contact information, such as phone number and email address.
  • Property ZIP code or general service location.
  • Pest concern, timing, property notes, and request details.
  • Consent confirmations submitted through website forms.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device type, pages viewed, and site usage signals.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to operate the website, process matching requests, communicate with users, improve site experience, and help connect homeowners with independent local provider options.

  • To respond to submitted pest provider matching requests.
  • To communicate by phone, email, or other provided contact method.
  • To help route request details to relevant independent provider options where applicable.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the website.
  • To comply with applicable legal, operational, or safety requirements.

4. Independent Providers

When you submit a matching request, relevant information may be shared with independent local providers or service partners for the purpose of responding to your request. Providers are independent from Pestora. Pestora does not control provider pricing, service terms, availability, licensing, insurance, or work quality.

Homeowners are responsible for verifying provider licensing, insurance, qualifications, pricing, scope, scheduling, warranties, and contract terms before hiring any provider.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

This website may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to remember privacy preferences, support basic website functionality, analyze site usage, and improve user experience.

For more information, please review our Cookie Policy.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to help protect information submitted through this website. However, no website, internet transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices

You may choose not to submit information through the website. You may also contact us to request updates or deletion of information where applicable and legally required.

If you do not want cookies or local storage used, you may adjust your browser settings or use the choices provided in the website policy banner.

8. Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for use by adults seeking pest control provider matching information. It is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
