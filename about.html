About Pestora

A warmer, clearer way to compare pest provider options.

Pestora is an independent provider matching platform built to help homeowners organize pest-related requests and compare local provider options before choosing.

Platform position

Pestora is designed for comparison, not direct pest control work.

  • Not a pest control company
  • No Pestora technicians
  • Independent local providers
  • Homeowners verify final details

Our model

Pestora helps homeowners compare options without pretending to be the provider.

Pest control needs can be stressful, but choosing a provider should feel more organized. Pestora helps homeowners share request details, understand the category they need, and compare independent provider options that may serve their area.

  • Submit one clear request with pest type, ZIP code, timing, and property notes.
  • Review local provider options based on category and availability.
  • Compare quote process, service scope, scheduling, and follow-up details.
  • Choose independently after verifying licensing, insurance, pricing, and terms.

Operating principles

Built around clear comparison and safe platform language.

Pestora avoids direct-service claims and keeps homeowners aware that local providers are independent. The final hiring decision belongs to the homeowner.

01

Aggregator-safe positioning

Pestora does not say it removes pests, performs inspections, sends technicians, or guarantees pest removal.

02

Transparent homeowner reminders

Homeowners should verify licensing, insurance, qualifications, pricing, service scope, and provider terms before hiring.

03

Useful request structure

Pestora encourages homeowners to share pest type, location, property details, timing, and comparison needs.

04

Independent provider choice

Pestora can help with provider matching, but homeowners decide which provider is right for their property.
